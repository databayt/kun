# Proposal — `higgs` cedes the text-carrying formats it also claims

**Status:** APPLIED 2026-08-06, with a correction — see "What was actually applied" below.
**Evidence:** bench-dispatch run `wf_95fff3f8-484` (audit CLEAN), corpus `fnv1a:5072a199`.

## The defect

`higgs`'s `when_to_use` contains both of these, in the same field:

> This is the **TEXT-FREE lane**: og images, infographics, testimonials, split comparisons
> and anything carrying copy render on **/carousel's template lane** instead.

> Triggers on: … make an ad, promo video, **og image**, **social card**, **hero image**,
> product shot, **mockup**, lifestyle scene, **moodboard**, showroom asset, brand kit …

It states the boundary and then claims the other side of it.

## How it surfaced

Three independent signals, none of which required an opinion:

1. **The one real miss in 402 cases.** `"banner"` → answered `higgs`, labelled `carousel`.
   The adversarial adjudicator confirmed the label (`labelIsCorrect=true`): `vocabulary.json`
   routes the `banner` spell to `/carousel` with effect *"Event or page-header banner —
   template lane at 1200x630, brand frame and logo included."*
2. **A prompt-collision** flagged by the extractor with no model involved: `"og image"` is
   claimed by the trigger lists of *both* `higgs` and `carousel`.
3. **`higgs` is the only skill in the fleet with `fp<>0` and `precision<1`** (0.9655, 1 false
   positive) — it is stealing, not being stolen from.

## Proposed change — trigger list only, prose untouched

Remove from `higgs`'s `Triggers on:` the four phrases its own prose assigns to `carousel`:
`og image`, `social card`, `hero image`, `mockup`. Keep the text-free lane intact
(`generate video`, `generate image`, `product shot`, `lifestyle scene`, `promo video`, …).

## This proposal cannot be self-approved

It edits a `Triggers on:` list, which is **the test set**. Guard 1 means applying it moves
`corpus_hash`, and `--check` will then refuse to compare scores across the edit. That is
correct and deliberate: the tuner must never be able to edit the cases it is scored against.

So this is a **baseline reset**, not a tuning win, and it needs a human decision on two things:

- Is `carousel` genuinely the right owner of og-images/social-cards/hero-images? The Portrait
  Gallery taxonomy says yes (text-carrying → template lane). The `higgs` prose says yes. Only
  the trigger list disagrees. Confirm the taxonomy is what you actually want.
- After applying, the baseline must be re-established and the reset noted in `weekly_history`.
  Scores before and after are **not comparable**.

## Expected effect — stated honestly

Small and mostly unmeasurable. It removes 4 of 402 cases and should fix 1 real miss. The
value is not the score; it is that `higgs` stops contradicting itself in the field the model
actually reads at dispatch time.

## What was actually applied — the proposal was wrong about half its list

Before applying, I checked the routing authority (`vocabulary.json` → The Portrait Gallery),
which assigns each format to a lane explicitly:

| spell | routes to | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `og image` | `/carousel` | link-preview card, 1200x630 template lane |
| `banner` | `/carousel` | event/page-header, brand frame + logo |
| `split-screen`, `infographic`, `testimonial` | `/carousel` | text-carrying archetypes |
| **`hero image`** | **`/higgs`** | *"Text-free hero shot — nano_banana_pro at 16:9"* |
| **`mockup`** | **`/higgs`** | *"higgs restyles a REAL product screenshot"* |
| `lifestyle`, `reel`, `showroom` | `/higgs` | text-free raster/video |

`hero image` and `mockup` belong to `higgs`. Removing them — as this proposal originally
proposed — would have introduced a defect while claiming to fix one.

**Applied:** dropped `og image` and `social card` from the trigger list; dropped
`banner, og image, social card` from the opening prose enumeration, which contradicted the
text-free rule in the very same field. **Kept** `hero image` and `mockup`.

The lesson generalises past this one skill: the proposal was assembled from the *symptom* (a
confusion between higgs and carousel) rather than from the *authority* (the spell→lane map).
A tuner that reasons only from failures will confidently propose changes in the wrong
direction, which is why the improvement lane routes every proposal through a human and why
none of them may self-apply.

**Effect on the corpus:** 402 → 401 cases, `corpus_hash` `fnv1a:5072a199` → `fnv1a:be5dc57d`.
Scores across that line are not comparable; the baseline reset is recorded in
`weekly_history`. The `og image` prompt-collision the extractor had been flagging is gone.
