# Edit

Claude Code edits video by **reading** it, not watching it. A word-level transcript of the
footage, a few KB of text, lets Claude choose cuts at word boundaries. It looks at a filmstrip
only when a decision needs it, then renders with ffmpeg. Everything runs on the Mac, so there are
no uploads and no API spend.

[record](/docs/record) and `shoot` **film** the product, [higgs](/docs/higgs) **generates**
clips with AI, and `edit` **finishes** them.

## Quick start

1. **One-time setup** (already done on Abdout's Mac):
   ```bash
   bash ~/.claude/skills/edit/scripts/setup.sh
   ```
   Then start a new Claude Code session so the HyperFrames plugin loads.
2. **Put the footage in a folder.** One folder per video, holding every take you want considered,
   for example `~/media/inbox/launch/`.
3. **Open Claude Code and say what you want**, in Arabic or English, with no slash commands:
   > _"rough cut ~/media/inbox/launch into a 60-second video with Arabic captions"_
4. Claude transcribes the footage and tells you what it found. It **proposes a plan and waits for
   your yes**, then renders a draft and shows you frames at the cut points.
5. Give feedback in plain words until it's right. The final file is overwritten in place.

## Keywords

Say any of these in a sentence and the edit lane runs. The full list is on the
[spellbook](/docs/keywords) under _The Pensieve_.

| Say | What happens | You get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `edit` · `مونتاج` | Claude asks what the footage is and what you need, then picks the lane | Whatever the lane below produces |
| `rough cut` · `قص الفيديو` | A transcript-driven cut that removes silences, fillers ("umm", "يعني") and false starts. When you repeat a line, the last take wins | `<folder>/edit/final.mp4` |
| `captions` · `ترجمة الفيديو` | Arabic captions burned in from the transcript, in Thmanyah Sans on a dark box | The same file, captioned |
| `title card` | An animated Arabic title, intro or outro card in the brand type and colors | A short MP4 card |
| `motion graphic` | A promo or explainer composed from code (HyperFrames), with no camera | An MP4 promo |
| `reel` | A vertical 9:16 version of a cut, captioned and capped at 58 s. With no footage it falls back to an AI clip (higgs), which only plans while credits are out | `reel.mp4`, 1080×1920 |

Example phrasings that route correctly:

| Arabic | English |
| --- | --- |
| _"مونتاج للفيديو في مجلد launch"_ | _"edit the clips in ~/media/inbox/launch"_ |
| _"قص الفيديو وشيل السكتات"_ | _"cut the silences out of this recording"_ |
| _"ترجمة الفيديو بالعربي"_ | _"add Arabic captions to final.mp4"_ |
| _"اعمل ريلز من الفيديو"_ | _"make a reel from the add-student demo"_ |
| _"كرت عنوان: إضافة طالب جديد"_ | _"title card for the admission flow"_ |

## Walkthroughs

### 1. Cut raw footage

> _"rough cut ~/media/inbox/launch — 60 seconds, Arabic captions, warm grade"_

1. **Transcribe.** Every take is transcribed locally with mlx-whisper, at about 5 s per 30 s
   of audio. Transcripts are cached and never redone.
2. **Read.** Claude reads `edit/takes_packed.md`, one line per phrase with its timestamps,
   and describes the material back to you.
3. **Plan.** It proposes what to keep, what to cut and the order, then **waits for your yes**.
4. **Draft.** It renders a fast draft and checks the frames on both sides of every cut.
5. **Final.** It renders `edit/final.mp4` at −14 LUFS loudness, with captions applied last. It
   reports the loudness numbers, because it can't listen.

Feedback that works: _"tighter"_, _"keep the joke at 1:20"_, _"drop the second example"_,
_"less warm"_, _"English captions"_, _"no captions"_, _"square for Instagram"_. Every change
re-renders from the cached transcript.

### 2. Captions only

> _"captions for ~/media/inbox/talk/final.mp4"_

Whisper sometimes misspells Arabic (`الأضافة` for `الإضافة`). Claude fixes the transcript before
burning the captions. Ask to see the caption file first if the wording matters, such as names,
brands or Quranic verses.

### 3. Title card or motion graphic

> _"title card: إضافة طالب جديد — بالقلم"_ · _"motion graphic announcing the admission season, 20 seconds"_

Claude writes an HTML composition: Thmanyah Serif Display headlines, Thmanyah Sans body text and
the clay accent `#e05638`. It runs the HyperFrames check, which covers contrast and layout, then
renders and shows you a frame. Ask for _"preview"_ to scrub it in the browser before the final
render.

### 4. A product demo with intro and outro

> _"demo video for add-student with an intro and outro card"_

The take comes from `shoot`'s sim. Its cached take is re-composed with no new filming. A `record`
screen recording also works. The cards come from HyperFrames, and everything is joined at a
constant 30 fps. The result overwrites `~/media/<repo>/<flow>/<flow>-ar.mp4`, with one
file per flow.

### 5. A reel

> _"reel from the launch cut"_

Claude takes the finished cut **without** captions, reframes it to 1080×1920 and burns the
captions last, placed above the TikTok, Instagram and Facebook buttons. There are two modes:
- **pad** (default for screen recordings): the whole frame sits over a blurred fill, so nothing is cut off.
- **crop**: a centered 9:16 crop, for talking heads.

## Get better edits

- **Record clean audio.** The transcript drives every decision, so a lapel or phone mic close to
  the speaker beats the laptop mic.
- **Flub a line? Pause, then say the whole sentence again.** The last take wins.
- **One folder per video**, with every take inside. Claude chooses across takes.
- **Say the target up front:** length, aspect (16:9, 9:16 or 1:1), captions yes or no, and the
  language.

## Where files land

| What | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Transcripts (cached) | `<folder>/edit/transcripts/*.json` |
| Transcript Claude reads | `<folder>/edit/takes_packed.md` |
| Edit decision list | `<folder>/edit/edl.json` |
| Captions | `<folder>/edit/master.srt` |
| Final video | `<folder>/edit/final.mp4`, overwritten on every re-render |
| Session notes | `<folder>/edit/project.md`, so the next session continues where this one stopped |
| HyperFrames renders | `<project>/renders/` |

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| `edit: only N GiB free (floor 10)` | Free disk space. Delete old `edit/` scratch, `renders/` drafts and `~/Downloads` media |
| Captions show boxes or broken letters | Thmanyah Sans is missing. Run `setup.sh`; it reports the font |
| Captions are missing entirely | The slim ffmpeg is on PATH. Use the scripts, which prepend `ffmpeg-full` |
| A HyperFrames video renders **black** | `dir="rtl"` is on `<html>`. Move `direction: rtl` onto each text element |
| A cut clips the first syllable | Whisper word starts drift after silence. Ask for _"more padding on the cuts"_ |
| `video-use: patch no longer applies` | Upstream changed. Re-port `helpers/transcribe_local.py` |

## Manual commands

The scripts live in `~/.claude/skills/edit/scripts/`. Claude runs them for you, but they also
work from a terminal:

```bash
S=~/.claude/skills/edit/scripts
$S/vu.sh transcribe_batch ~/media/inbox/launch --language ar   # local transcription
$S/vu.sh pack_transcripts --edit-dir ~/media/inbox/launch/edit  # → takes_packed.md
$S/vu.sh render <edit>/edl.json -o <edit>/final.mp4 --build-subtitles
$S/reel.sh final.mp4 reel.mp4 --srt <edit>/master.srt --mode pad
cd <project> && $S/hf.sh check && $S/hf.sh render              # HyperFrames
auto-editor long.mp4 --margin 0.2s -o long-cut.mp4             # quick silence strip
```

## Under the hood

| Tool | Role | Why this one |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [video-use](https://github.com/browser-use/video-use) (MIT) | Transcript → EDL → render, with a self-check at every cut | Works from a ~12 KB transcript instead of frames |
| `mlx-whisper`, large-v3-turbo | Arabic and English transcription with word timestamps | Fast on the M4, with no upload and no spend. It replaces video-use's paid ElevenLabs Scribe through a local patch |
| [HyperFrames](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes) (Apache-2.0) | Compositions written as HTML and GSAP, rendered to MP4 | Chrome shapes Arabic correctly, the same model `shoot`'s motion lane uses |
| `ffmpeg-full` (Homebrew, keg-only) | Encodes and burns captions | The slim `ffmpeg` has **no libass**, so it can't burn subtitles. `ffmpeg-full` sits beside it, and `shoot` and `record` stay untouched |
| `auto-editor`, `yt-dlp` | Quick silence cuts; pulling in video from a URL | |

**Not used, on purpose:**
- **Remotion.** A company of 4 or more people needs its Company licence ($100/mo minimum), and HyperFrames covers the same ground for free.
- **ElevenLabs Scribe.** It is a paid API.
- **ffmpeg, Final Cut Pro and DaVinci Resolve MCP servers.** Skills that call the CLI cost fewer tokens, and the workflow is fully agentic, with no desktop editor.

What `setup.sh` does:
- Installs the Homebrew formulas and `mlx-whisper`.
- Clones video-use to `~/oss/video-use` and applies `.claude/skills/edit/patches/video-use-local-asr.patch`.
- Symlinks the clone as the `video-use` skill and installs the `hyperframes@hyperframes` plugin.

The first transcription downloads about 1.5 GB of model weights.

## House rules

- **One file per piece, always overwritten.** Never `-v2` or `-final2`.
- **Disk floor: 10 GiB.** The render scripts refuse below it. Scratch is purged after delivery.
- **Plan before render.** The strategy is confirmed before the first full render.
- **Measured, not claimed.** Audio is reported as loudness numbers.
