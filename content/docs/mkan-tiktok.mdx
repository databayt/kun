# Mkan on TikTok

**@mkan.rw is Mkan's front door.** In Kigali people look for a home to rent on TikTok, where brokers post
a video per house. Mkan's page shows those homes cleaned up, one home per video, and sends the viewer to
WhatsApp, where Mkan's assistant finds homes, checks them with their brokers and books the viewing. Mkan
earns 10% of the broker's commission when the lease is signed.

The page has one job: **turn a viewer into a chat.** Reach matters only because it feeds that.

```
viewer ──► watches a home ──► writes on WhatsApp (the code on the post) ──► homes ──► viewing ──► lease
```

This page is the standing guide. The running log of what was tried and what it did is
`mkan/docs/tiktok-playbook.md`; the account, number and domain setup is [Mkan Business Setup](/docs/mkan-setup).

## Where the page stands

| Number | Now | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Videos on the page | 72 | |
| Followers | 25 | +20 a week |
| Comments, ever | 0 | 5% of viewers like, comment or share |
| Shares, ever | 4–6 | |
| Views per post, recent reposts | 60–110 | 200 |
| Chats traced to the page in a month | 2 | 1% of views |

Two things stand out:

- **Volume did not grow the page.** From 7 November every fresh ad was posted, 3 to 15 a day. Seventy-two
  videos later the page has 25 followers and has never had a comment. The spread-out posts averaged 69
  views against 139 before, and the change was rolled back on 9 November.
- **The few viewers who wrote were not carried through.** One pasted the link of our own post and got a
  generic search back; one picked a home and was told a person would reply. Both gaps were closed on
  14 November (a TikTok link now opens its home; the assistant answers and books by itself).

So the next gains come from fewer, better videos and from answering every viewer, not from posting more.

## Who watches, and what they need

People moving within Kigali or to it: families, students near the campuses, professionals near Kacyiru and
Kimihurura, and people abroad arranging a home for relatives. Most read English, many prefer Kinyarwanda,
some French. They watch muted as often as not. They have been burned by rental scams and by brokers who do
not answer. What makes them write:

- the **price and the area in the first second**, on screen;
- a home that looks **real and still available** (posted today or yesterday);
- **one easy step**: send a code on WhatsApp, the viewing is free.

## What the page posts

| Kind | What it is | Share |
|---|---|---|
| **One home** | A broker's video with their consent, cleaned of added text and logos with no blur, 12–25 seconds, the best room first | Most posts |
| **What a budget gets** | "What 300k a month gets you in Kigali": three or four homes from our own facts | 1–2 a week |
| **An area** | "Renting in Kimironko": prices, what is near, two homes | 1 a week |
| **How renting works** | Months up front, deposit, commission, the free viewing, how to avoid a scam | 1 a week, pinned |
| **An answer** | A real question from a viewer answered as a video | When there is one |

Why not only broker reposts: TikTok does not allow reposting someone's material without permission, and
can leave a video out of the For You feed without removing it. Guides add that a repost needs something
of your own on top to be recommended, which TikTok has not confirmed. Mkan has each broker's yes, and adds
its own: the clean cut, the price and area, the check that the home is still free.

### The video itself

- **Vertical 9:16, 1080 × 1920.** TikTok accepts larger files, but most sources say it plays at 1080p;
  a 4K master is a test (ten posts against ten), not the default.
- **First second:** the best room, with the price and area on screen: "2 bedrooms · Kimihurura · 400k a
  month". On-screen text is read by search, so it is the hook and the keyword at once. (Putting text on
  the video is Abdout's call; since 5 November videos carry none and the facts sit on a text-only card.)
- **12–25 seconds**, so most people finish. Finishing and rewatching count for more than likes.
- **Sound** from TikTok's Commercial Music Library, the original muted. A business account may only use
  that library.
- **Nothing of the broker's on screen:** no number, no handle, no logo. A phone number filmed in the scene
  cuts that shot. A video whose own text says the home is taken is never posted.

### The caption

1. **Line 1, what people search:** "House for rent in Kimihurura, Kigali: 2 bedrooms, 400,000 RWF a month".
2. **Line 2, the one step:** the WhatsApp number and "send K5811". It must show before "more".
3. Three to five hashtags: `#kigali` `#houseforrent` `#inzuikodeshwa` `#rwanda` and the area.

Words people type, to use in captions and on screen: *house for rent Kigali*, *apartment Kigali*,
*inzu ikodeshwa*, *inzu yo gukodesha i Kigali*, *maison à louer Kigali*, and every neighbourhood by name
(Kimironko, Remera, Kacyiru, Kibagabaga, Gisozi, Kicukiro, Kagarama, Gikondo, Rebero, Nyamirambo, Kanombe,
Nyarutarama, Kimihurura).

## When, and how much

- **The best 3 to 5 homes a day**, not every ad: posted in the last 48 hours, a clear price and area, a
  clean render, and a broker who answers.
- **Evenings**, 17:00 to 21:30 in Kigali, with a morning post; never two in a row from the same broker.
- **Never more than 15 a day**, TikTok's limit for automated posting.

## From viewer to chat

| Door | What happens |
|---|---|
| **The code** ("send K5811") | Opens that home in the WhatsApp chat, with two like it |
| **The post's link**, pasted into WhatsApp | The same: the link names the home (since 14 November) |
| **The bio link** | Opens mkan's page of the homes from our videos; chats from it are counted apart |
| **"On TikTok", "saw your video"** | Counted as TikTok's even with no code |
| **A direct message on TikTok** | Not answered today. See below |
| **A comment** | Answered by a person, never automatically |

**Direct messages.** Three ways exist, in this order:

1. **TikTok's own automatic messages**, once the page is a verified Business account: a welcome message
   (250 characters, reviewed by TikTok before it goes live) and keyword replies that fire on an exact word.
   Set in the app under Business Suite, Message center. The welcome message points to WhatsApp and the
   code. No code to write.
2. **The Business Messaging API**, for which Mkan applies in parallel. It answers inside a 48-hour window
   and cannot send the first message. Vendors say it is closed to accounts registered in the EEA, the UK
   and Switzerland and needs advanced access; availability for Rwanda is to be confirmed with TikTok. When
   it is open, the same assistant answers there.
3. **Driving the inbox through a browser** is not done: it risks the page.

## Paying for reach

Organic first. Money is spent only to push a video that already works:

- Promote a post **only after** it beats 200 views and 5% interaction on its own.
- Use Promote's **Get leads → Other messaging apps → WhatsApp**, so the tap opens the chat. Guides put
  the minimum near $3 a day; the app shows the real figure. People without WhatsApp are left out.
- One post, three days, one audience (Kigali, 20–45). Judge on **chats per dollar**, not views.
- Every spend is Abdout's decision first.

## The numbers, and the weekly read

Collected every evening from the page and from TikTok Studio: followers, and per post views, likes,
comments, shares, saves, watch time and completion. Read once a week:

1. **Which three posts did best, and what did they share?** (area, price band, first frame, length, hour)
2. **Which step lost the most people?** views → profile visits → chats → homes → picks.
3. **One change for the coming week**, tested on ten posts against ten, kept only if it wins. A change
   that cuts views per post by more than 20% is rolled back.

The scorecard on `/monetize` shows the same numbers with the weakest one marked.

## How it runs

| Step | When | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Sweep | 06:00 and 15:00 | Finds brokers' new rental videos, reads the facts, files them in the CRM |
| Clean | Before each slot | Removes added text and logos with no blur, checks the result, rejects what cannot be cleaned |
| Pick | Morning and evening | Chooses the day's homes; only brokers who said yes |
| Post | The slots above | Uploads through TikTok Studio with the caption and a library sound |
| Count | 21:00 | Reads the page's numbers into the ledger |

Everything lives in `mkan/scripts/crm` (`tt:pick`, `tt:render`, `tt:upload`, `tt:stats`). The scripts post
and count; they never reply to people.

## Rules the page keeps

- **A broker's video only with their yes.** Recorded per broker; a no is final.
- **No broker's number, handle or logo** reaches a viewer. Mkan stays in the middle.
- **Real prices, real homes.** A home a broker says is taken leaves the page's queue at once.
- **The search for new videos never uses the page's own login.** A limit on that session would stop the page.
- **AI is labelled** where TikTok asks for it.
- **A person answers comments.**
