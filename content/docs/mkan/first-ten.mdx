# The first 10 deals

**Everything runs by itself; Abdout watches it and goes to every viewing.** Until 10 deals are paid, every
step of every deal reaches his WhatsApp as one line, the line books viewings inside his hours, and he goes to
each one with the tenant. No step waits for his approval. After the 10th paid deal, the live lines fold into
one day line in the 20:00 message (decided 2026-11-15).

His number for all of it is **+249 919 071 294** (`MKAN_REP_WHATSAPP`). Brokers pay Mkan's 10% to **MoMo
0780 984 777** until Databayt Ltd has a merchant code.

## What reaches his phone

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| Every deal step | One line: a tenant picked a home, the broker was asked, the broker said yes (and how fast), a viewing time is proposed, viewed, signed, paid, lost, or a chat went quiet |
| 07:00 Kigali | Today's viewings |
| 20:00 Kigali | Tomorrow's viewings; on Sunday also the week's numbers |
| 2 h before a viewing | A reminder with the meeting point and the tenant's number |
| 3 h after a viewing | "How did K5811 go?" |

Each viewing in the schedule carries the time, the home and its rent, the meeting point, the tenant (name,
number, language), the broker, the terms to confirm, **Mkan's share to agree (10% of the broker's
commission)**, a one-tap *Add to calendar* link, and three questions to ask the tenant:

1. How did you find your last home?
2. What did a broker charge you?
3. What almost stopped you from taking a home?

Write each answer back with `note K5811 …`: they are what the first 10 deals are for.

Viewings are booked 09:00–18:00, Monday to Saturday, with at least 90 minutes between two of his.

## The numbers, and what each means

Real people only (the team, test phones and personal contacts are left out). The week's numbers come every
Sunday at 20:00 and on the word `numbers`; the First 10 tile sits on [/monetize](https://www.mkan.sd/en/monetize).

| Number | What it means | Act when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Real WhatsApp tenants a week | Demand reaching the line | The pivot gate: 20 by **2026-11-29**, with at least one viewing |
| Shown → picked | The homes fit what people ask | Under 20%: stock or price mismatch |
| Picked → broker yes, median hours | Brokers answer | Over 24 h: the next broker |
| Yes → viewing booked | The step where every tenant stopped so far | Under 50%: call the tenant |
| Booked → viewed | Tenants show up | Under 70%: confirm the day before |
| Viewed → signed | The video matched the home | Low: the posts or the prices mislead |
| Signed → paid, days | Brokers pay the share | Any over 7 days: chase it |
| Net per deal (share − trip cost) | Whether a visit is worth it | Below zero: lean to homes of 700k and up |
| Paid deals, of 10 | When the loop stops reporting each step | — |
| Chats that quote a K-code | TikTok brings tenants | None for 2 weeks: change the video, not the count |

## Twenty is the record

The Mkan agent writes every tenant and every deal into Twenty's `mkan` workspace (`mkan.databayt.org`)
within minutes:

- **Deals:** one card per deal, its stage moving from Picked to Paid or Lost, with the rent, the commission,
  Mkan's share, the trip cost, the terms and the notes.
- **Tenants:** each real person who wrote, with where they came from (a TikTok code, the bio page, Today, the
  web) and how far they got.
- **Tasks:** one per booked viewing, assigned to Abdout, due at the viewing.

An edit in Twenty acts like a word from his phone. Moving a deal to *Signed* sends the share request, and
moving it to *Paid* records the money. A tenant marked *opted out* gets nothing more. A broker's relationship
or share reaches the line at once. If Twenty is down, nothing stops: the writes wait and go when it is back.

- in both chats: "today", "what's new", «اليوم», "uyu munsi" list the day's homes, numbered on WhatsApp with
  the /today link, and /find has a *Today's homes* starter card.
