# Mkan — where we stand (2026-11-15)

**The bot works. The tenants are missing.** In four weeks we built a full rental agent: web chat,
WhatsApp, broker outreach, a deal pipeline, and a line that books viewings by itself. In 30 days it
had one real WhatsApp tenant, no viewing and no deal. The wall is at the top of the funnel, not the
bottom.

> **Update, 11-15 evening.** Main is deployed: the viewing loop, the trust gate (732 homes public, every one
> with someone to contact) and the video homes are live, and the 12 Nov tenant was answered. Abdout took step 3
> below his way: he is in the loop for the first 10 deals, gets every step and a viewing schedule on his phone,
> and goes to every viewing. Twenty is now the record for tenants and deals, and the day's TikTok homes post
> to the web as Today on Mkan. See [The first 10 deals](/docs/mkan/first-ten).

Sources: `pnpm mkan:read funnel` and read-only prod SQL, run 11-15; `www.mkan.sd/api/health`; the
mkan git log; the session notes on the rental agent, /monetize and the TikTok page.

## What is built (10-17 to 11-15)

- **/find web agent**: a chat in the ChatGPT-iOS layout, with Claude Haiku reading and writing.
- **WhatsApp line** on Cloudflare: the same agent answers tenants on the business number.
- **Broker directory** (238 brokers) with gated outreach. Brokers are asked about a home only when a
  real tenant picks it.
- **Deal pipeline**: deal sheet → viewing booked → reminders → signed → paid → revenue entry.
- **Viewing loop**: the line agrees the time with the broker and books the viewing itself.
- **/monetize**: a scorecard, a road to break-even, and an autonomous sprint runner.
- **TikTok**: enhanced home videos auto-posted to @mkan.rw.
- **Trust gate**: only homes with someone to contact stay public.

About 30 deploys in four weeks.

## What the numbers say

Real people only. The team, test phones and personal contacts are filtered out.

| Stage, last 30 days | Chats | From the stage before |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wrote to Mkan | 32 (31 web, **1 WhatsApp**) | |
| Were shown homes | 30 | 94% |
| Picked a home | 8 | 27% |
| A broker was asked | 7 | 88% |
| The broker said yes | 4 | 57%, a median 25 h later |
| Named a viewing time | **0** | 0% |
| Viewings, deals, revenue, ever | **0** | the `Deal` table is empty |

- **Volume.** Most real chats came in launch week (10-21 to 10-23). Over the last two weeks there
  were 0 to 2 chats a day, the team included.
- **TikTok.** 76 posts in two weeks and 2,677 views, but **no comment ever**, and no chat has ever
  quoted a home code. As posting rose from 21 to 55 a week, views per post fell from 46 to 31.
- **Deploys.** Production runs `5d37d76`, from 11-09. Everything since then is on main and not live:
  - the 11-14 viewing loop;
  - the fix for the real tenant left with "a person will check" on 11-12;
  - the one-word deal close;
  - the trust gate;
  - the video homes.

  That is six days of work no tenant has seen.
- **Cost.** About 80,000 RWF ($55) a month. A deal earns Mkan 5,000–10,000 RWF, so break-even needs
  9–16 paid deals a month, or 3–5 after the container cut ([cost study](https://github.com/databayt/mkan/blob/main/docs/cost-study-2026-11.md)).

## What is strong

1. **The bot itself works.** It answers in 1.3 s at the median, with no errors in 7 days. The Claude
   reader scores 98–99%. The models cost about $1–2 a month, inside the plan's included credits.
2. **We measure honestly.** Since 11-08 every number leaves the team out, and `mkan:read funnel`
   reads each chat's furthest stage and who it waits on. Most startups never get here.
3. **Supply is solved.** Mkan has 4,687 Airbnb homes and about 700 broker homes, and fetches about 70
   fresh broker ads a day.
4. **Mkan stays in the middle.** No phone numbers change hands, which protects the cut.

## Where we disagree

**A. We keep building conversion on top of no demand.** The 11-09 note already said that "a funnel
fix with no traffic moves nothing." Then on 11-14 we built the viewing loop, the one-word close, the
after-viewing steps and the quiet-tenant nudge, for four tenants. The wall is the top of the funnel:
one WhatsApp tenant in 30 days.

**B. Full autonomy is premature.** No human in the loop is the right end state but the wrong way to
get the first 10 deals. All four tenants who got a broker's yes stopped before naming a time. Four
phone calls would have told us why; another week of code cannot. Under *The Mom Test* (canon #7),
we have not yet asked one Kigali renter how they last found a home.

**C. TikTok — settled with Abdout.** On TikTok, Mkan is a marketplace: a real-time feed of homes
that finds tenants through video. So the house posting is the core of the page, and it stays on.

- **Kept, never switched off:** the daily posting of homes, each video enhanced first, exactly as
  the lane runs today.
- **Tuned:** at most 5 posts in any 24 hours, set from the numbers below.
- **Added on top:** other kinds of video: informative videos (how renting in Kigali works, prices
  by neighbourhood, what a deposit covers), stories, and tenants' and brokers' experiences. These
  are additions to the home posts, never a replacement for them.

**How many a day — decided from our own numbers (11-15).** Every post at least a day old (59
posts), split by how many we had posted in the 24 hours before it:

| Posts in the 24 h before | Posts | Median views | Got 50+ views |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 | 10 | 157 | 10 of 10 |
| 4–5 | 4 | 122 | 4 of 4 |
| 6–7 | 12 | 6 | 0 of 12 |
| 8+ | 33 | 7 | 0 of 33 |

TikTok shows our first five posts in a day to people and buries the sixth onward. Neither the hour
nor the gap between posts explains the difference; the count does. Over-posting also buries the
next morning's posts: 11-10 to 11-12 posted 29 videos for about 200 views in total, while 11-13
posted 2 that drew 423.

- **The rule:** at most **5 posts in any 24 hours**, every kind of video counted, at 07:00, 11:30,
  15:30, 18:30 and 20:30. The house posts never stop; only the count is bounded.
- **The loop:** every week the 21:00 stats job re-measures the curve on posts at least 48 hours old.
  If the 5th post of a day still draws at least half the views of the first ones, the cap rises by
  one for a week to test the 6th. If the last allowed post draws under a quarter, the cap falls by
  one. The cap stays between 3 and 8, moves one step a week, and each change is posted to #mkan.
  `pnpm tt:cadence` prints the curve at any time.
- **Still open:** which number decides success after reach is fixed: comments, or chats that quote
  a home code. Once the other kinds of video exist, how to split the 5 between them and the homes.

**D. The unit economics are thin and fragile.**
- 10% of a broker's commission comes to 5,000–10,000 RWF. One trip to a viewing can eat that, and a
  Mkan person goes to every viewing.
- The broker pays after the tenant already has the home. That is the easiest payment to skip: "Call
  host" stays on listing pages, and broker numbers show in their own videos.
- The tenant, the side with the pain, pays nothing.

**E. One person is the bottleneck.** Waiting on Abdout today:
- the deploy;
- `MKAN_REP_WHATSAPP` and `MKAN_PAYOUT_MOMO`;
- a dedicated business number (the line still carries personal chats);
- the go-ahead to restart the sprint runner (parked since 11-09, mkan#65);
- a native Kinyarwanda read of the new lines.

The automation makes work for Abdout faster than he can clear it.

**F. Focus.** The Drive is cash flow first, and the north star is paying schools. Hogwarts bills its
first client on 12-19. Mkan has taken a large share of the engine's time for four weeks and earned
0 RWF. That is fine for an experiment with an end date, and not fine without one.

## What to do next

1. **Deploy main today.** It is built and carries the fix for the real tenant.
2. **No new automation until the first paid deal.** Only fix what is live.
3. **Do the first 10 deals by hand.** Call the four tenants who got a broker's yes, go to the first
   viewings in person, and log what each one cost and why it stalled.
4. **Five renter interviews this week, Mom-Test style.** Ask "Walk me through how you found your
   current place": where they looked, whom they paid, and how long it took.
5. **TikTok stays a marketplace.** House posting keeps running every day (at most 5 posts in 24 h), each home enhanced
   first. Informative, story and experience videos come on top of it. Each post
   asks a question that invites a comment. Facebook and WhatsApp housing groups are a second
   channel where Kigali renters already ask. Step 2's freeze does not touch this lane.
6. **Test who pays.** Pick one and run it on the next 10 tenants: a small tenant fee through MoMo
   (for a viewing, or for "we find it for you"), or a per-lead fee charged to brokers up front. Lean
   toward homes at 700k RWF or more, where the same work earns three times as much.
7. **Set a pivot-or-persevere date (*The Lean Startup*, canon #6).** By **2026-11-29**: at least 20
   real WhatsApp tenants and at least one viewing done. If not, Mkan goes to maintenance (scraping and
   the bot stay on, no new builds) and the time moves to Hogwarts.
8. **Cut the container after demand shows.** Leaving it saves about 44,000 RWF a month, but only
   after tenants show up.
