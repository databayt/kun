# Hogwarts — Social

Modern school management for Saudi / MENA schools — *"schools automate the mundane."* Hogwarts is
the flagship [SaaS](/docs/social#business-models--who-were-talking-to) product: the sale is **B2B**
(school owners and administrators buy it), but the reach is **B2C** (parents and teachers see it
every day, and their trust closes the sale). Social has to speak to both.

## Positioning

- **Business model:** SaaS.
- **Type:** B2B sale · B2C social proof.
- **Tagline:** Modern school management for Saudi / MENA schools.
- **Angle:** give administrators their time back; give parents visibility and calm.
- **Market for this period:** **Sudan (Khartoum)** — where both pilots are. The product still
  targets Saudi/MENA; only the social run is Sudan-first. Register stays **rung 2** (فصحى معاصرة
  مبسّطة, the house default — see [Copy](/docs/social/copy)), which reads correct to a Sudanese,
  Saudi, and Emirati reader at once, so nothing written now has to be rewritten when the market
  widens.

## Audience

| Segment | Role | What moves them |
|---------|------|-----------------|
| **School owners / administrators** | The buyers (B2B) | Time saved, fewer errors, billing/attendance handled, compliance |
| **Teachers** | Daily users | Less admin, easier grading/attendance |
| **Parents** | Reach + trust (B2C) | Visibility into their child, timely communication |

## Channel mix

> **This period: Facebook only, Sudan-first (2026-08-06).** Hogwarts is the whole of the
> [one-slice decision](/docs/social/status#scope--one-slice-on-purpose-2026-08-06) — one brand, one
> channel, one country — until the loop is proven end to end. The table below is the **target mix**,
> not the running one. Instagram stays complete behind its flag, Telegram stays two env variables
> from live, and LinkedIn stays Ali's personal profile rather than an API.
>
> Sudan is where the evidence is: **King Fahad Schools, Khartoum** has been on the pilot since
> 2026-04-15, and the second pilot (Albayan) is Khartoum too. It is also why static Arabic cards
> lead over video here — intermittent connectivity favors light formats. The moral code's Sudan
> clause binds every post: **humility in tone; never exploit crisis for engagement.**

Parents and school communities in MENA live on Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp; the buyers are
reachable on LinkedIn. That's the core four.

| Priority | Channel | Why |
|----------|---------|-----|
| **Primary** | **LinkedIn** | Reach the decision-makers — owners, principals, education buyers |
| **Primary** | **Facebook**, **Instagram** | Where parents and school communities are; visual proof, announcements |
| **Primary** | **WhatsApp** | School↔parent communication is already WhatsApp — broadcast lists, updates. *Primary by audience value, `manual` by transport* — no posting API, so `/publish` hands out a copy-out block |
| **Secondary** | **X**, **Telegram** | Announcements, education-tech conversation |
| **Low** | TikTok, Snapchat | Occasional light content; not the buyer's channel |

## Content pillars

1. **School success stories** — a school that cut admission or billing time; real numbers, with
   permission. The B2B proof that also reassures parents.
2. **Feature highlights** — attendance, grades, billing, SIS — each shown solving one concrete
   headache. Short demo clips (`/higgs` media).
3. **Saudi / MENA education context** — term calendars, regulator realities, Arabic-first.
4. **Administrator time-savings** — "an hour of paperwork, done in a click."

## Facebook playbook — the focus channel

Facebook is the one live channel (2026-08-01), so it carries the whole cadence until Instagram
unlocks. Four post archetypes rotate; each maps a [content pillar](#content-pillars) to an
[asset type](/social/media) and a publish lane:

| Archetype | Pillar | Asset type (lane) | Publish lane |
|-----------|--------|-------------------|--------------|
| **Feature highlight** | Features / time-savings | `og` card, 1200×630 (template — 1-slide deck) | review queue → now or drain |
| **Scene + story** | Parent visibility, classroom life | `hero`/`story` photo (higgs, from the [library](/social/media)) | review queue → now or drain |
| **Swipe story** | Product intro, module ecosystem | `carousel` deck 4:5 (template) | attach the slides → review queue → drain (2–10 as one carousel post) |
| **Context / trust** | MENA education, open source | `og` or `split` card (template) | review queue → now or drain |

**The weekly rhythm:** Monday 07:00 the seed lane files two briefs from
`content/social/pillars.json` (ISO-week rotation over the pillars — the Calendar tab shows which
two, and **Queue now** files one off-cycle); the 5-minute drain answers them AR-first on the Max
pool and attaches matching library media; the full draft lands in the **review queue** on the
Publish tab; a human fine-tunes and approves, either publishing straight away or scheduling to
the drain. Numbers come back via `/measure` (platform insights six-hourly + PostHog UTMs).
Nothing publishes without the human tap — the gate is the design, not a bottleneck.
