# Balqalam — Social

*بالقلم — مدرستك كلها في مكان واحد.* Balqalam is **the** school management brand. The product
used to be called Hogwarts; that name is retired from everything public, and the `hogwarts` brand
in this Hub is hidden and carries no briefs. Every school post goes out as Balqalam, on the
[Bilqalam بالقلم Facebook Page](https://web.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61592684345321).

The strategy behind this page is *Balqalam Marketing Strategy v2* (2026-09-27). This page is the
part of it the social pipeline executes: `/calendar` and the Monday seeder read the briefs in
`content/social/pillars.json`, `/draft` reads this page for voice and claims, the media lane reads
each brief's `visual`, and `/publish` delivers to the Page after a human approves.

## Goal

Social is a **trust asset for sales**: a school owner checks the Page before a demo, and an active,
calm, Arabic-first Page removes doubt. The company metric is active paying schools; the Page's own
signal is **messages from decision-makers** (owners, principals, registrars, finance leads), not
followers, likes or reach.

## Positioning

- **Business model:** SaaS, per-school subscription. B2B — the owner signs.
- **Language:** **Arabic first.** Plain modern standard Arabic (rung 2); English is the mirror.
- **Message:** your whole school in one place — admission, attendance, fees, results and family
  communication — in an Arabic system that starts from the data the school already has.
- **Markets:** one Page, two tracks. **Sudan** is the proof track (the live pilot, the warm
  network); the **Gulf** (Saudi, UAE) is the cash track. Knowledge posts are written for any
  Arabic-speaking school; a post that talks price, payment or regulation names one country.

one persona and every ask points at the decision-maker.

## Cadence and calendar — the editorial queue

Balqalam does **not** use the weekly seeder. Its posts come from an **editorial queue**: ninety
numbered, undated drafts written ahead of time from the product itself, loaded into
`/social/publish` in order. The team takes the next one whenever it posts — daily, or with gaps;
nothing is dated, so nothing slips.

| Piece | Where |
|-------|-------|
| The drafts (`001`–`090`, Arabic first, English sibling) | `content/social/queue/balqalam/NNN-<slug>.md` |
| The plan — 13 themed weeks × 7 slots, each tied to a feature | `content/social/queue/balqalam/plan.json` |
| The drafting contract | `content/social/queue/balqalam/SPEC.md` |
| The product facts each draft came from | hogwarts `<feature dir>/SPOTLIGHT.md` (named in each draft's frontmatter) |
| Loader (strict craft gate, idempotent) | `node scripts/social-drafts.mjs queue --brand balqalam [--dry]` |

The seven-slot rhythm, repeated: a school-day **pain** · a **product proof** · one **persona's**
view · **learning science** (evidence labelled) · **trust** · a short **how-to** · a **question**
to owners and principals. Evenings (19:00–23:00 local) are the window.

To write the next ninety: add rows to `plan.json`, write `091-…` onward against `SPEC.md`, run the
loader with `--dry`, then without. Replace or extend the set at a quarterly review rather than let
it repeat unexamined.

## Voice

- Arabic is **written**, never translated. A concrete school scene before any abstraction
  (`content/social/scenes.json` → `balqalam`).
- Intelligent, direct, calm. No hype words (ثورة، يغيّر كل شيء، الأول بلا منافس), no corporate
  filler, no emoji beyond one.
- One persona, one idea, one ask per post.
- **The ask has no link.** Close by inviting a message to the Page (راسلونا على الصفحة) —
  `copy.mdx` check 6: "if there is no link, say what to do instead". One ask, last.
- Evidence posts separate three layers and never blend them: what the study found · what it may
  mean for a school · what Balqalam actually does. Label evidence strong, promising, preliminary
  or unsupported. No brain imagery, no dopamine, no learning styles, no correlation as causation.

## Claims

The canonical registry is `hogwarts/content/docs-en/marketing-brief.mdx`. A draft that needs a
number, result or feature not listed as allowed there is refused, not softened.

**Allowed:** about 28 capability areas and about 60 features · Arabic is the default (2,247 Arabic
strings) · King Fahad Schools, Khartoum, as the one live pilot — no results, no quotes without
recorded approval · Free up to 100 students and 10 teachers · Excel import with Arabic headers ·
opens in the browser and installs to the home screen · data exports as CSV or JSON · built for
slow connections · the MENA-10 pilot: three months free, then an annual contract.

**Never:** the Hogwarts name or wordmark · hours or money saved, percentages, uptime · "paying
customers" or any school count beyond the one pilot · offline · native or store app · advanced
analytics · payment gateways by count · automatic submission to ADEK or any regulator ·
compliance with PDPL, GDPR or FERPA · a student's face without written consent.

## Media

- **Idea posts** carry a `visual` in their brief (`plate` or `lifestyle`): calm school settings,
  desks, rooms, hands — **no faces of children**. The media lane compiles it against the brand kit
  (`content/media/brand-kit.json` → `balqalam`); the mark is the quill, never the Hogwarts wordmark.
- **Proof posts** carry no generated visual on purpose. They attach a **real** Arabic screenshot
  of the shipped screen — the hogwarts repo root holds `*-ar.png` captures, and `/record` makes
  fresh ones — never a mocked or generated interface.
- Text-bearing cards (lists, carousels) render on the template lane (`/carousel`), not as images.

## Measure and kill

`/measure` reads each post at 24 hours, 7 days and 30 days. Record who engaged (owner, teacher,
parent, student), not just how many. **Four weeks without a single message from a decision-maker
means change the angle, not the volume.** Boost nothing until the week-8 review shows which posts
bring decision-makers.

## Wiring

| Piece | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Product id | `balqalam` (env suffix `BALQALAM`) |
| Facebook Page | **Bilqalam بالقلم** — [profile.php?id=61592684345321](https://web.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61592684345321) |
| Graph Page id | `1218016808068580` — **not** the `61592684345321` in the profile URL, which is the profile number and returns `error_subcode: 33` from Graph |
| Env | `FACEBOOK_PAGE_ID_BALQALAM`, `FACEBOOK_PAGE_ACCESS_TOKEN_BALQALAM` |
| Meta app | `Gabriel` (874547138717755) — the same Business app that carries the other Pages; renamed from "Hogwarts Social" 2026-08-19 so the app name is brand-neutral across four Pages |
| Domain / UTM | `balqalam.com` |

The Page token is minted the same way as the others: a **user** grant on the Business app with the
Balqalam Page ticked in Meta's granular-consent dialog, exchanged for a long-lived user token, then
`/me/accounts` for the Page token — which must come back with `expires_at: 0`. A permission grant made before this Page existed does **not** cover it: the granular scopes name
page ids explicitly, and clicking **Generate Access Token** in the Graph API Explorer silently
re-issues the *old* grant rather than offering the new Page. The step that actually adds it is
**Edit settings → Select all → Continue → Save** in the login dialog. Do not revoke the app to
force a fresh dialog — that would invalidate the three working permanent Page tokens.

Wired and verified 2026-08-19: `checkFacebookHealth("balqalam")` returns *Bilqalam بالقلم*, and
the hogwarts / mkan / databayt tokens survived the re-consent unchanged.
