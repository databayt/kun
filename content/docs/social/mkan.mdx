# Mkan — Social

*"Homes for rent, simplified" — Mkan for homes, in Sudan and Kigali.* A rental marketplace, Airbnb-style. This
is the most **visual, consumer-first** brand in the portfolio: the [SaaS](/docs/social#business-models--who-were-talking-to)
product sells to two sides — **B2C** renters/travelers looking for a place, and **B2B** hosts
listing one. Social leans hard into imagery and short-form video.

## Positioning

- **Business model:** SaaS marketplace (two-sided).
- **Type:** B2C (renters) + B2B (hosts / property managers).
- **Tagline:** Homes for rent, simplified.
- **Angle:** beautiful places, booked in a tap — and effortless for hosts.

## Audience

| Segment | Side | What moves them |
|---------|------|-----------------|
| **Renters / travelers** | Demand (B2C) | Gorgeous listings, trust, easy booking, good prices |
| **Hosts / owners** | Supply (B2B) | Bookings filled, low hassle, fair fees |
| **Property managers** | Supply (B2B) | Manage many units in one place |

## Channel mix

Mkan is a photo-and-video product. Instagram, TikTok, and Snapchat carry it; Facebook and X
support. This is the brand where the consumer-visual channels finally lead.

| Priority | Channel | Why |
|----------|---------|-----|
| **Primary** | **Instagram** | Property showcases, Reels, stories — the natural home |
| **Primary** | **TikTok**, **Snapchat** | Short property tours; huge MENA/Saudi reach |
| **Secondary** | **Facebook** | Marketplace crossover, host community, older renters |
| **Secondary** | **X**, **Telegram** | Deals, new-listing drops, community |
| **Low** | LinkedIn | B2B host acquisition only |

## Content pillars

1. **Property showcases** — the hero content. Text-free `/higgs` imagery + real listing photos;
   Reels/tours on IG/TikTok/Snapchat.
2. **Booking made easy** — the "tap to book" story; trust and reviews.
3. **Host success** — "listed on Monday, booked by Friday." Recruits supply.
4. **Saudi destinations & lifestyle** — chalets, getaways, seasonal travel — inspiration that sells.

## Kigali TikTok page

Kigali's monthly rental market lives on brokers' TikTok pages: the WhatsApp number in the bio and on screen,
one short video per house, the price and area on screen. Mkan runs the same
play on its own page — beside, not inside, the Airbnb-style site.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Account** | [`@mkan.rw`](https://www.tiktok.com/@mkan.rw), personal (creator) account — Abdout's existing account (was `@abdout982`; login +250 780 984 777, on his iPhone). `@mkan` belongs to someone else. Kept personal on purpose: a Business account is limited to the commercial music library (no trending sounds), and the in-app switch offered no Rwanda option. The bio link unlocks at 1,000 followers; the WhatsApp number on screen does the job until then |
| **Bio** | `Houses in Kigali / +250780984777` — no "verified" claim until homes are checked; a bio link unlocks at 1,000 followers (`https://www.mkan.sd/en/find`) |
| **WhatsApp** | +250 780 984 777 (`MKAN_KIGALI_WHATSAPP`) — on every end card and caption |
| **Inventory** | Monthly rentals only: owners' and field-agent homes, Airbnb hosts who took a monthly price, and broker leads once that broker said YES (Twenty host `repostConsent`) |
| **Cadence** | 3 videos a day (`TIKTOK_POSTS_PER_DAY`) |

**The daily queue** lives in mkan (`scripts/crm/tiktok-post.ts`, launchd
`com.databayt.mkan-tiktok-post` at 07:00, after the 06:00 broker sweep):

1. `tt:pick` — today's homes, spread across districts; a posted home rests 30 days.
2. `tt:render` — one branded 9:16 video per home, in three passes:
   - **Source** — for a broker lead, that broker's own TikTok video (yt-dlp, cached in
     `.data/tiktok-post/src/`); a photo post or a deleted video falls back to a slideshow of the
     home's photos with a slow push-in.
   - **Enhance** (ffmpeg-full, the [Media Studio](/docs/media) build) — vidstab stabilising,
     light denoise (`hqdn3d`), a gentle grade (`eq`), `unsharp`, lanczos to 1080×1920 (a
     landscape clip sits on a blurred fill), muted, cut at 45 s.
   - **Brand** — mkan's brand kit (`content/media/brand-kit.json` → `production.mkan`): an ivory
     sting with the arch mark, the mark + "Video @broker" credit top-start, a slate lower third
     (price, place, rooms, ref) that slides up and hands over to a price pill at 7 s, and a slate
     WhatsApp end card. Everything sits inside TikTok's safe area.
   - Delivered through `media.sh encode reel` (BT.709, faststart, 60 s cap); `--slideshow` forces
     the photo path. No music — the poster adds a trending sound in the app.
3. `tt:card` — each video + its caption (English, one Kinyarwanda line, hashtags) lands in
   Slack #mkan (CDN link when the bot can't upload files).
4. **A person posts it** from the phone: save → TikTok → sound → paste caption → post, then
   `pnpm tt:posted <ref> <url>`.
5. `tt:stats` reads views back with yt-dlp; `tt:ask` lists the brokers, the consent message
   (EN + Kinyarwanda) and records ASKED / YES / NO.

Inquiries arrive on WhatsApp quoting the ref; the operator finds the home on `/find` or in the
CRM. Posting by API waits on TikTok's Content Posting API audit (posts stay private until it
passes) — the phone step goes away then.

Mkan creative production runs on three core playbooks:
