# Mkan — Social

*"Homes for rent, simplified" — Mkan for homes, in Sudan and Kigali.* A rental marketplace, Airbnb-style. This
is the most **visual, consumer-first** brand in the portfolio: the [SaaS](/docs/social#business-models--who-were-talking-to)
product sells to two sides — **B2C** renters/travelers looking for a place, and **B2B** hosts
listing one. Social leans hard into imagery and short-form video.

## Positioning

- **Business model:** SaaS marketplace (two-sided).
- **Type:** B2C (renters) + B2B (hosts / property managers).
- **Tagline:** Homes for rent, simplified.
- **Angle:** beautiful places, booked in a tap — and effortless for hosts.

## Audience

| Segment | Side | What moves them |
|---------|------|-----------------|
| **Renters / travelers** | Demand (B2C) | Gorgeous listings, trust, easy booking, good prices |
| **Hosts / owners** | Supply (B2B) | Bookings filled, low hassle, fair fees |
| **Property managers** | Supply (B2B) | Manage many units in one place |

## Channel mix

Mkan is a photo-and-video product. Instagram, TikTok, and Snapchat carry it; Facebook and X
support. This is the brand where the consumer-visual channels finally lead.

| Priority | Channel | Why |
|----------|---------|-----|
| **Primary** | **Instagram** | Property showcases, Reels, stories — the natural home |
| **Primary** | **TikTok**, **Snapchat** | Short property tours; huge MENA/Saudi reach |
| **Secondary** | **Facebook** | Marketplace crossover, host community, older renters |
| **Secondary** | **X**, **Telegram** | Deals, new-listing drops, community |
| **Low** | LinkedIn | B2B host acquisition only |

## Content pillars

1. **Property showcases** — the hero content. Text-free `/higgs` imagery + real listing photos;
   Reels/tours on IG/TikTok/Snapchat.
2. **Booking made easy** — the "tap to book" story; trust and reviews.
3. **Host success** — "listed on Monday, booked by Friday." Recruits supply.
4. **Saudi destinations & lifestyle** — chalets, getaways, seasonal travel — inspiration that sells.

One post an hour from 07:00 to 22:30, each the broker's own video with their text stripped (since 11-07; was 3 a day) |

**The daily queue** lives in mkan (`scripts/crm/tiktok-post.ts`), run by the Hermes cron
`mkan-tiktok-post` at 07:00, after the 06:00 broker sweep. It picks every postable ad from today
and yesterday (yesterday's evening ads only reach mkan in the 06:00 sweep), renders the first and
posts it. `mkan-tiktok-upload-retry` (08:30–17:30) and `mkan-tiktok-upload-evening` (18:30–22:30)
run at :30 past every hour. Each renders and posts its share of the day, so the page spreads
evenly whatever the day's count, and a missed slot is caught up two at a time. The evening slots
first add the ads the 18:00 sweep fetched. The 07:00 run wakes the session-vault Chrome first
(its launchd job respects a Cmd-Q, so a quit or a reboot leaves it down), and a slot that finds
nothing rendered runs the whole morning post (Neon refused it twice on 10-24, 10-25, 10-27 and
10-28, and the page posted nothing those days). All of them run detached from the Hermes gateway, so a
gateway restart can't kill them, and share one lock, so they never upload at once:

1. `tt:pick` — first records brokers' answers to the repost ask that came in on the WhatsApp line
   (see [Broker consent](#broker-consent)), then picks today's homes, spread across districts; a
   posted home rests 30 days.
2. `tt:render` — one branded 9:16 video per home, in three passes:
   - **Source** — for a broker lead, that broker's own TikTok video (yt-dlp, cached in
     `.data/tiktok-post/src/`); a photo post or a deleted video falls back to a slideshow of the
     home's photos with a slow push-in.
   - **Enhance** (ffmpeg-full, the [Media Studio](/docs/media) build) — vidstab stabilising,
     light denoise (`hqdn3d`), a gentle grade (`eq`), `unsharp`, lanczos to 1080×1920 (a
     landscape clip sits on a blurred fill), muted, cut at 45 s.
   - **Brand** — mkan's brand kit (`content/media/brand-kit.json` → `production.mkan`): an ivory
     sting with the arch mark, the mark + "Video @broker" credit top-start, a slate lower third
     (price, place, rooms, ref) that slides up and hands over to a price pill at 7 s, and a slate
     WhatsApp end card. Everything sits inside TikTok's safe area.
   - Delivered through `media.sh encode reel` (BT.709, faststart, 60 s cap); `--slideshow` forces
     the photo path. mkan's own AI music bed goes under every render, so the AI-generated label
     is switched on for every post.
3. `tt:carousel` — one **data carousel** a day (photo mode, 7 slides): a cover, five homes —
   price, place, rooms, the ref, the WhatsApp line — and a call-to-action slide, in the brand
   kit. Built from CRM facts only, no broker photo or footage, so every Kigali monthly home
   qualifies. The caption and the end slide say Mkan checks the home is still free and takes the
   tenant to see it, viewing fee on Mkan — Mkan stays in the middle and never hands out the
   broker's number (11-01). Themes rotate by
   day (district → budget → bedrooms); a shown home rests 14 days. Slides land on the CDN
   (`mkan/uploads/tiktok-post/<date>/carousel-…`) with the caption in #mkan.
   **Picture video** — `tt:datavideo` turns the same carousel into a short video: the homes'
   photos (or hand-picked Commons area photos) on the beat of mkan's own AI music bed, no voice.
   It goes out every day with the AI-generated label on, and so do the slides themselves, as a
   swipeable photo post.
4. `tt:card` — each video + its caption (English, one Kinyarwanda line, hashtags) lands in
   Slack #mkan (CDN link when the bot can't upload files).
5. `tt:upload` posts every piece through TikTok Studio's web upload in the session-vault Chrome
   (127.0.0.1:9222, logged in as @mkan.rw): caption pasted, AI label on, 1.5–3.5 min between
   posts, `uploads.json` per day so nothing posts twice. A logged-out vault says so in #mkan.
   The slides go through Studio's Photos tab (it takes up to 35): the post's title is the
   caption's first line, no AI label (no voice on stills), and it waits for every slide —
   Studio wakes Post while photos still upload — so a partial set is never posted.
   `TIKTOK_PHOTO_POSTS=off` skips it.
6. `tt:stats` reads every video on the page back with yt-dlp — views, likes, the last 7 days, the
   best — into one #mkan line every Monday at 10:00 (`mkan-tiktok-stats`) and a snapshot in
   `page-stats.jsonl` (11-01: 6 videos, 977 views, 32 likes). `tt:ask` lists the brokers and the consent message
   (EN + Kinyarwanda + FR) and posts the asks to #mkan as wa.me links for the phone to send.

Inquiries arrive on WhatsApp quoting the ref: the line opens that home first and, on the
tenant's yes, Mkan checks it with the broker and sets up a three-party viewing (broker messages
are off until `pnpm wa:brokers gated`; until then a person picks it up from #mkan). TikTok's Content Posting API (kun#168) replaces the Studio upload once its audit passes.

### Broker consent

The CRM holds every broker video the sweep found (`tiktokPost`, 931 on 11-01) — links, not files.
**A broker's video goes on the page only after that broker said YES** (Twenty host
`repostConsent`): reposting someone's video without permission is a copyright claim, and
takedowns would kill the page. On 11-01 no broker was recorded as asked, YES or NO.

- **Record a YES** — `pnpm tt:consent --yes=<handle,…> --source="<how they said it>" --apply`
  sets the host's `repostConsent`, adds a note saying where the answer came from, and keeps the
  broker's videos on the Mac and the CDN, so a post never depends on TikTok still having them.
  From Slack: tell Hermes "these brokers said yes: a, b, c" (Abdout only).
- **Answers on the WhatsApp line record themselves** — the repost ask goes from the phone, so the
  line's inbox notes it when it sees it; while it is the latest question, a broker's reply answers
  it (YES / YEGO / OUI, NO / OYA / NON; anything hedged goes to a person) instead of an open "is this
  available?". Each answer gets a #mkan card, and the next 07:00 run records it on the CRM host.
- `pnpm -s mkan:read crm consent` shows who said what.
