**Kun (كن) is a token-efficient code machine that collapses the time between idea and shipped product.**

You know how traditional development and business automation work: you stitch together disconnected tools, operate custom server infrastructure, and juggle separate platforms for coding, planning, marketing, and sales.

This approach breaks down for small, fast-moving teams who need to stay cash-flow positive and turn ideas into paying customers.

This is what **Kun** solves. It transforms Anthropic's product suite alongside Antigravity, OpenCode, and Hermes into a single, unified company OS. It is built around the following principles:

- **Configure Over Build:** Pure markdown and JSON. Zero custom orchestration servers to operate.
- **Dual Pipelines:** One continuous pipeline for software delivery (`idea → ship`) and one for multi-channel brand momentum (`calendar → measure`).
- **Universal Capability:** Every workstation gets the full fleet of 47 agents, 68 skills, 25 MCPs, and 37 rules—the machine is the unit of power.
- **Product Truth:** Autonomous videography (`record`) and real-world pilot execution over synthetic marketing fluff.
- **Financial Discipline:** Subscription-only burn ($500/mo cap), cash-flow first, and open-source shared economics (SSPL).

## Dual Execution Pipelines

### 1. Feature Pipeline · *Code to Production*

```
IDEA ──► SPEC ──► [PLAN ──► TASKS] ──► SCHEMA ──► CODE ──► WIRE ──► CHECK ──► SHIP ──► WATCH
                  └── human gate ──┘
```
*Trigger: `feature <name>` chains all 9 stages; each stage is also an independent skill with its own exit gate.*

### 2. Social & Media Pipeline · *Product to Audience*

```
CALENDAR ──► DRAFT (AR-first) ──► ASSETS (Media/Record/Higgs) ──► APPROVE (/social/publish) ──► PUBLISH ──► MEASURE
```

- **[Social Automation](/docs/social)**: Multi-channel broadcasting across 5 brands & 8 platforms.
- **[Product Videography (`record`)](/docs/record)**: Autonomous OTP handling, ad-grade video cuts, and screenshot archives in `~/media/` synced to Google Drive.
- **[Media Studio (`higgs` · `carousel`)](/docs/media)**: Generative image/video marketing & bilingual slide decks.

## Core Principles

1. **Configure over build** — Don't rebuild what Anthropic ships.
2. **Architecture over vibe coding** — Humans design, AI generates within strict rules.
3. **Guardrails as training data** — Every clean component enforces the next.
4. **Full spectrum** — The engine runs the business, not just the code.
5. **Open source, shared economy** — SSPL public repos, sustain through real-world value.
