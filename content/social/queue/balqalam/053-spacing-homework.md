## en

Cramming feels like learning. That feeling is the problem.

Research on spacing found that practice spread across days and weeks is remembered longer than one long session of the same total time. The evidence is strong in laboratory studies; the Education Endowment Foundation's review calls it promising in classrooms, where fewer trials exist.

For a school, that could mean shorter homework that returns to last month's topic, instead of one heavy assignment the night before a test.

Balqalam won't design the spacing — the teacher does. What it does is carry it out: set each assignment with a due date, every student in the class is notified, gets a reminder the day before, and parents see the same dates.
