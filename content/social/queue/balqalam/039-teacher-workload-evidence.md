## en

Ask teachers what wears them down, and many won't mention teaching first.

The evidence: in the OECD's TALIS survey of teachers, respondents in many countries put too much administrative work among the leading sources of stress in their job. Strong as a record of what teachers say — but it is a survey, and it cannot show that less paperwork lifts results.

What it may mean for a school: know what each teacher already carries before adding the next task. A load nobody can see is rarely shared fairly.

What Balqalam does, and doesn't: it counts every teacher's weekly periods from the actual timetable and marks each one under-used, normal or overloaded. That is teaching periods only — not admin time, and not a verdict on anyone's teaching.
