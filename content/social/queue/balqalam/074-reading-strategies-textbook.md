ما وجدته الأبحاث: تضع مؤسسة الوقف التعليمي البريطانية EEF تعليم استراتيجيات الفهم القرائي بين أعلى ما راجعته أثرًا، بكلفة منخفضة، والدليل عليه واسع وقوي. المقصود أن يتعلم الطالب صراحةً كيف يتوقع ما سيأتي، ويسأل النص، ويلخّص الفقرة، ويتوقف عند ما لم يفهمه. يؤدي المعلم الاستراتيجية بصوت مسموع أولًا، ثم يسلّمها للطلاب شيئًا فشيئًا.

## en

A pupil reads the whole page, and can't tell you what it was about.

The evidence. The Education Endowment Foundation (EEF) rates teaching reading comprehension strategies explicitly as one of the highest-impact, lowest-cost approaches it has reviewed, on extensive evidence — strong. That means showing pupils how to predict, ask questions of a text, summarise a paragraph and stop at what they didn't understand: the teacher models it aloud, then hands it over gradually.

What it may mean for a school: the strategies are taught on the texts pupils actually read, not in a separate lesson. Teachers plan better with the chapter in front of them.

What Balqalam does — and doesn't: it won't teach comprehension for you. It puts the curriculum in place. For the Sudanese curriculum, each subject carries the official textbook with its chapters and lessons laid out, and teachers can add a worksheet or question to a lesson.
